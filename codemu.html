CODEMU #1

We aren't just partying during Springfest; we're also putting our algorithmic minds to the test! CODEMU is a rapid-fire coding marathon where you need to write the most optimized and accurate code for algorithmic problems of varying difficulty levels within a strict time limit.

Form Your Team: True to the festival spirit, teams will consist of a maximum of two members.

AI is Forbidden: This is a battle of pure analytical skills between you and your teammate; the use of artificial intelligence is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification.

Speed is Key: In this scoring system, just solving it correctly isn't enough! The faster you upload your code to the system, the higher your score will be thanks to the "Speed Multiplier".

Invest in Your Career: This competition boosts your algorithmic thinking skills, serving as the perfect preparation for your future technical interviews.

Special Festival Prizes & Benefits

1st Team: 3-Month Claude Pro Membership 2nd Team: A Coursera credit worth $40 3rd Team: 1-year ACM Student Membership
